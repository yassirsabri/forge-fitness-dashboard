Day 1: The Power Foundation

Focus: High mechanical tension to optimize motor unit recruitment across prime movers.

Resistance: Low (Level 3–5) | Cadence: 80–90 RPM.

Anaerobic Sprints (6 Cycles: 30s Work / 30s Recovery)

• 30s Burst: Increase resistance to High (Level 12–15+). Target 100+ RPM with maximum neural drive.

• 30s Reset: Drop resistance to minimum. Maintain fluid, light revolutions to clear metabolic waste.

Kinematic Safety Matrix

Biomechanical threat classification and mechanical tension management metrics.

Exercise BlockRisk LevelMitigation & Biomechanics
Seated Leg PressMedium-HighBringing knees past a 90-degree angle under load can result in spinal flexion or excessive pelvic tilting. Keep range cleanly monitored.
Hanging Knee RaisesMediumOver-reliance on dynamic swinging engages the hip flexors excessively instead of keeping isolation tension focused on the lower abdominal sheath. Maintain zero momentum.
Standing Calf RaisesLow-MediumEgo-lifting with extreme loads induces structural compression along the spinal cord. Keeping knees micro-flexed prevents joint stress pooling.
